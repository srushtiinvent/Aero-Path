AeroPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

AeroPath currently stores temporary interface choices in your browser. When account features are enabled, account and trip records will be stored in the configured database, while uploaded boarding passes require a separate secure file-storage service.

Third-party services

Flight and hotel searches are sent to the configured data providers. AeroGuide questions are sent to OpenAI only when AeroGuide is configured. Do not include sensitive personal information in chat messages.
